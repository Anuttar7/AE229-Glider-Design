# AE229 Glider Design Project

## Overview

This repository contains the report and design work for **AE229: Glider Design** project. The project focuses on designing, testing, and evaluating lightweight gliders, with particular attention to lift, drag, glide performance, stability, and practical flight behaviour.

We explored two glider designs:
-   **Glider 1** --- an airfoil-based wing design intended to improve lift and glide performance.
-   **Glider 2** --- a simplified design developed after evaluating the sensitivity and practical limitations of Glider 1.

The report includes design considerations, flight testing, wind-tunnel measurements, lift-to-drag calculations, and a drag-polar analysis. \[Report, pp. 1--10\]

## Project Structure

The report is organized around the following topics:

1.  Glider 1: design and fabrication
2.  Lift-to-drag (L/D) calculations
3.  Wind-tunnel testing
4.  Drawbacks of Glider 1 design
5.  Glider 2: design and fabrication
6.  Drag-polar analysis

## Key Results

The main experimental and design outcomes reported by the team are:

- Glider 1 achieved an estimated **C<sub>L</sub>/C<sub>D</sub> of 4.06** from the flight-test calculation.
- Wind-tunnel testing showed increasing lift from **15 gm at -5°** to **76 gm at 10°**, followed by a slight reduction to **74 gm at 15°**.
- The airfoil-based configuration produced higher lift but was found to be sensitive to small imbalances.
- Glider 2 adopted a simpler Depron construction with adjustable elevator geometry.
- The reported drag-polar data show a reduction in **C<sub>L</sub>/C<sub>D</sub> from 5.12 to 3.08** across the measured lift-coefficient range.
